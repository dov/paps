Plain Pango markup works as usual, e.g. <b>bold</b> and <i>italic</i>.

<p indent="-18" left="18" align="justify">1. A hanging indent: the first line starts at the margin and the following lines are indented by 18 points. This paragraph is also justified, so the right edge is straight.</p>
<p indent="-18" left="18" align="justify">2. A second item. Every <tt>&lt;p&gt;</tt> ends at its own end of line, so the settings do not leak into the next line.</p>
